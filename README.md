# Laikn: Carbon-Aware Code Router 🌿

Laikn is an experimental assistant designed to help developers consider the environmental impact of their compute tasks. By analyzing real-time weather patterns and grid data, Laikn suggests the greenest available data centers for your next job.

> **Note:** Laikn provides routing **suggestions** based on current environmental factors. It does not automatically move or execute your cloud workloads, keeping you in full control of your infrastructure.

## Features

*   **Real-Time Analysis:** Fetches live wind, solar, and temperature data to find the best region right now.
*   **Physics-Based Logic:** Uses hub-height wind speeds (80m) and cubic power formulas for accurate renewable energy estimation.
*   **Global Reach:** Monitors 17 major cloud regions across North America, Europe, Asia, South America, and Australia.
*   **Analytics Dashboard:** A private dashboard to track your potential carbon savings over time.

## Getting Started

To find the optimal path for your next task, you can:
*   **Right-click** anywhere in your editor and select **Laikn: Eco-Route**.
*   **Click the Laikn icon** in the bottom status bar of the editor.

The extension will ask which regions you have access to and provide a detailed analysis of the most sustainable options.
